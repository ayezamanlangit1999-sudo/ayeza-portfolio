Real work, real tools

Two problems I noticed — and built my way out of.

I don't just execute tasks — I look for the recurring manual step and ask whether it needs to be manual at all. Here's what that's looked like in practice.

Case Study 01

Automated Weekly Statement Distribution

The Problem

Some customers request a Statement of Account weekly — beyond what our standard automated dunning system covers — which meant pulling and sending those manually every week.

What I Built

An AI-assisted, Outlook-based automation that generates and sends the weekly SOA to each relevant customer automatically.

The Outcome

Eliminated the recurring manual step entirely — those statements now go out automatically, every week, with no follow-up needed on my end.

Tools: Excel · Outlook · AI Tools

Case Study 02

Multi-Document Invoice Compliance Automation

The Problem

One account has no dedicated processing team, so its invoicing falls fully on me. Every invoice needs three additional documents merged with it — a Bill of Lading copy, a Purchase Order, and a Certificate ID — each unique per invoice, sourced from separate customer platforms across a multi-day approval cycle, then printed and mailed. In peak periods, that's batches of up to 50 invoices at once, done fully by hand.

What I Built

Company policy restricts installing software outside the approved app store, so off-the-shelf PDF tools weren't an option. I used AI assistance to build a custom merger in Jupyter Notebook that combines each invoice's unique document set into one compliant packet, working entirely within approved tools.

The Outcome

Replaced document-by-document manual merging with an automated step, cutting prep time per invoice packet even as batch volume scales into dozens at once.

Tools: Jupyter Notebook · Claude AI
